Pi Recitation Challenge

Welcome to the Pi Recitation Challenge! This is one of multiple ventures of Cam's Memory Project, which has been made publicly available as an interactive game.

Rules of the game are simple: recite pi digits one-by-one. A full history of the recited digits will be available. You will have up to 3 lives, whereby inputting an incorrect digit will cost you one life.

In Sprint Mode, players will specify a time limit, during which the goal is to recite as many correct digits as possible.

In Survival Mode, players will specify a digit limit (my website handles up to 2000 digits so far). Individual split times will be provided for every 100 digits.

Best of luck!!
